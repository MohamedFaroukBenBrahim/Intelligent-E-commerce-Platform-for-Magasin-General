Our story

From a single store in 1883 to a nationwide network — here's how MG became part of Tunisian everyday life.

Founded in 1883, Magasin Général (MG) has grown from a small trading post into Tunisia's most trusted retail institution. For over 140 years, we have been at the heart of Tunisian households, providing quality products at accessible prices.

Today, MG operates 82 stores across 23 governorates, making it one of the most geographically distributed retail networks in North Africa. Our mission remains unchanged: to serve every Tunisian family with the products they need, every day.

With the launch of Freshy26, our digital platform, MG takes a bold step into the future — combining our rich heritage with modern e-commerce technology, AI-powered recommendations, and a seamless online shopping experience.

Our journey

MG opens its first store in Tunisia, marking the beginning of a retail legacy that would last over a century.

National expansion begins

Following Tunisia's independence, MG expands its network across the country, becoming the go-to retail destination.
